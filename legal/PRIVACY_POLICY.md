## 1. What we collect
- **Account:** phone number and/or email, username, display name, age band, avatar, bio.
- **Age status:** the fact that you confirmed you are 18+ (and any age-assurance result).
- **Content:** pins, photos, comments, reactions, messages, meetups, whispers, stories, reports.
- **Location:** approximate location when you post or opt into presence. We store an exact
  point privately to compute your chosen precision, but **only the fuzzed/approximate
  location is shown to others.** Live presence is opt-in.
- **Usage & device:** app interactions, device/OS info, IP address, and diagnostics.
- **Cookies/local storage:** to keep you signed in and remember preferences.

## 2. How we use it
To provide and secure the Service; match meetups by age band; show nearby content; power
messaging and notifications; enforce our Guidelines and prevent abuse/fraud; comply with law;
and improve the product. Legal bases (GDPR): performance of contract, legitimate interests
(safety, anti-abuse), consent (e.g. precise location, push), and legal obligation.

## 3. When we share it
- **With other users:** what you choose to post, at your chosen precision/visibility. Meetups
  are anonymous until mutual reveal.
- **Service providers (processors):** hosting/database (Supabase), maps (CARTO/MapLibre),
  SMS/auth, storage, analytics, moderation vendors — under data-processing terms.
- **Safety & law:** to respond to legal requests, protect users, or report illegal content
  (e.g. CSAM to NCMEC/authorities as required).
- We do **not** sell your personal data.

## 4. Location specifics
- Exact coordinates are never shown to other users. Photos have EXIF/GPS stripped on upload.
- You can turn presence off, choose per-post precision, and meetups are always block-level at
  public venues.

## 5. Retention
- Stories: ~24 hours. Meetup chats: with the meetup (+ short buffer). Expired pins: archived
  then purged per [RETENTION SCHEDULE]. Reports/moderation and safety logs: kept as needed for
  safety and legal reasons. Account data: until deletion + a grace period.
