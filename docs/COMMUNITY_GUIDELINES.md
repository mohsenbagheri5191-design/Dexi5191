# Pin Drop — Community Guidelines

Pin Drop is a live map of your neighbourhood. Keep it useful, kind, and safe.
Break these and your content may be removed and your account restricted or banned.

## You must be 18+
Pin Drop is an adults-only community. No exceptions.

## Be a good neighbour
- Post real, helpful, local things: events, tips, sightings, lost & found, hazards.
- No spam, scams, ads, or repetitive posting.
- No harassment, hate speech, threats, or targeting individuals.
- No sexual content, nudity, or anything sexualising minors. Zero tolerance.
- No illegal activity, weapons/drug sales, or dangerous misinformation.
- Don't post other people's private information (no doxxing).

## Location & privacy
- Don't post someone's home or exact private location.
- Don't use pins or meetups to track, stalk, or surveil anyone.
- Respect "invisible" — don't try to work out where someone is.

## Meetups (18+)
- Meet in public. Tell a friend where you're going. Leave whenever you want.
- Treat people with respect. No pressure, no harassment.
- Anonymous until you *both* choose to reveal — honour that.
- Report anyone who makes you uncomfortable. Block is always one tap away.

## Emergencies
- Pin Drop is not an emergency service. In a real emergency, call your local
  emergency number.
- Don't post false emergencies — it puts people at risk.

## Reporting
See something off? Use **Report** on any pin, meetup, message, or profile.
Serious safety concerns are prioritised. We may remove content and act on accounts.

_These guidelines may change as the community grows._
